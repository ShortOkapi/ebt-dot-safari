EBT Dot Safari

A long-awaited tool for EuroBillTracker map dot hunters.

Ferran (Tuscac, from Girona 🇪🇸) was the latest of several EBTors over the past few years to ask for live GPS tracking on their personal Google Dotmaps.
Miguel Viterbo (lmviterbo, from Lisboa 🇵🇹) had the nerve to create and direct the project, with all code generated by Gemini, and final audit by ChatGPT.
The tool was rigorously audited, optimized, and field-tested by veteran developer Peter Zagler (elpeza s̶p̶a̶s̶i̶b̶a̶, from Linz 🇦🇹), with analysis assistance from Claude.
