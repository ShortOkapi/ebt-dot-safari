Help and instructions ↗

Target areas

The radar looks for dots that are unconquered and eligible in your selected target area. Choose Target area in the expanded radar panel; your choice is remembered on this device.

  • Euro-use area: curated dots on land where euros are used or widely available, excluding known unconquerable dots.
  • All land worldwide: dots marked as containing land by OpenStreetMap data and manual corrections. Access, safety and euro availability are not individually verified.

Places in your nearest target

With a usable GPS position and your Notes loaded, use Places in target dot [row, column] on the GPS panel or in your current-dot popup. It refers to the radar's chosen target, identified by the list heading. The places-panel target-area selector also changes the radar setting, recalculates the target and retains a chosen destination when it still belongs to the same target dot and qualifies for that model. Choose a suggested place or a point on the map, check the Selected destination box, then explicitly open it in Google Maps. Distances are straight-line distances from your location. After a temporary GPS loss or returning to the app, the panel recovers on a fresh usable fix and rechecks your choice. The target has a yellow halo; a waiting GPS reading is labelled as last known.

Suggestions currently cover Portugal and selected test dots, including Calais. English names appear beneath local names when supplied by OSM. A dot outside the trial coverage is distinguished from a failed download and an empty list. Previously loaded place data can be used offline; Google Maps and background maps may still need a connection.

Euro-use includes the Eurozone, other countries and territories using the euro (including Kosovo and Montenegro), and Switzerland. Prepared country outlines check manually chosen points locally in the covered test dots. Foreign or uncertain points show a warning with a choice to proceed or choose another point. Country outlines may include territorial waters and do not verify dry land, access or safety. Place data: © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL.

Before you set off

Dot Safari cannot guarantee euro banknotes in any eligible dot. We do our best to classify dots correctly, but maps can contain mistakes and conditions can change. Check access and safety before setting off, and respect local restrictions.

No dot is worth entering a dangerous or forbidden area. And if your next conquest appears to require a submarine, please question the map before buying one.

Help improve the Euro-use map

Everyone is welcome to suggest corrections through EBT Target Map ↗. Your edits stay in your own working copy. Send a Change Report to Miguel / lmviterbo for review, or simply message him with the dot’s [i, j] reference and your reasoning. Ordinary saved edits do not change the published map.
